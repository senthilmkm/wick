Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Local-First Health & Motion Data

Wick is built on a strict local-first architecture. Your Apple HealthKit step counts, CoreMotion pedometer deltas, accelerometer tilt angles, sleep schedule, and Wax Ledger history stay exclusively on your iPhone and are never uploaded to our servers, sold, or used for advertising.

2. Microphone Usage

When Live Breeze Shake or Mic Blow-Out at Bedtime is enabled, Wick reads microphone decibel levels locally only while the Flame screen is open and active. Audio is never recorded, saved, or transmitted—and you can disable microphone access anytime in the Sanctuary tab or iOS Settings.

3. iCloud CloudKit Circle & Spark Rescue

When you opt in to pair a friend via Apple iCloud Shared Database (CloudKit) or send a Spark Rescue link, only your display name, candle state (e.g., Blazing, Flickering), flame intensity, and streak count are shared—never your raw step counts or HealthKit records.

4. On-Device Keychain & Subscriptions

Wick stores your 3-day reverse trial start date and weekly free-tier quotas locally in the iOS Keychain. Paid subscriptions are processed securely by Apple StoreKit 2.
