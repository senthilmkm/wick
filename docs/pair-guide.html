Both Friends First

Turn on iCloud on both iPhones

Open iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud and make sure iCloud is signed in on both phones. That is how your candles talk safely without accounts or passwords.

1
What You Do

Tap "+ Pair Widget" in the Circle tab

Open Wick, tap the Circle tab at the bottom, and tap + Pair Widget. Send the iCloud invite link to your friend by text message (iMessage or WhatsApp).

2
What Your Friend Does

Tap the link you sent

Your friend taps your link. Their iPhone opens Wick automatically — now your friend can see your candle flame and streak in their Circle!

3
For Two-Way Pairing

Your friend sends their link back to you

Now your friend opens their Circle tab, taps + Pair Widget, and texts their link to you. Once you tap it, you see their candle too!

4
Both Friends

Add the Wick Widget to your Lock Screen

Long-press your iPhone Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → Add Widgets → Wick. Now you can check your flame anytime without even unlocking your phone.

5
Save Each Other!

Send a Spark when a flame is fading

If your friend's candle starts smoking, they tap Send Spark and text you the link. You tap it and walk 500 steps — and their candle automatically relights on their iPhone!

Good to know: You can pair 1 friend for free. Upgrade to Wick Pro (one-time lifetime unlock) to pair unlimited friends!
