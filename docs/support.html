Wick Support

Need help with Apple Health, Lock Screen Widgets, or Spark Rescue? Find quick answers below.

1. My candle isn't lighting in the morning

Open iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Wick and ensure Steps is enabled. Wick lights automatically after your first 5 steps once your Wake Schedule begins.

2. Does Wick work with Apple Watch?

Yes. Wick uses Apple HealthKit as its single source of truth, automatically merging iPhone and Apple Watch steps without double-counting.

3. How does Night Mode & Mic Blow-Out work?

When iOS Sleep Focus turns on—or when you long-press your candle and blow into the bottom microphone—decay freezes for the night and your day's wax hardens into a permanent streak band.

4. What is a Glass Dome?

If you're sick or traveling, spend 300g of earned wax (or an owned Glass Dome) to freeze your candle for 24 hours without losing your streak.

5. How do Multiplayer Lock Screen Widgets sync?

Friend widgets sync via Apple iCloud Shared Database. Ensure you are signed into iCloud in iOS Settings so your Circle widgets stay updated.
