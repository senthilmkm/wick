Wick Support

Need help with Apple Health, Lock Screen Widgets, Glass Domes, or Spark Rescue? Find quick answers below.

1. How do I light my candle in the morning?

Tap the pocket lighter on the Home screen or take your first 5 steps of the day. Ensure Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Wick → Steps and Motion & Fitness are enabled.

2. Does Wick work with Apple Watch and update live?

Yes. Wick streams live step-by-step updates via CoreMotion while open and uses Apple HealthKit as its single source of truth to merge iPhone and Apple Watch steps without double-counting.

3. Why does blowing shake the flame during the day without putting it out?

During daytime hours, blowing gently makes the flame flutter like a real breeze. Wick only extinguishes into Night Mode on a strong blow during your configured bedtime window (or when you long-press the candle → Blow Out for Night).

4. How do I drop or lift a Glass Dome?

Long-press the candle to drop a Glass Dome (24-hour rest freeze that also shields the flame from wind). To remove it early, long-press the candle and tap Lift Glass Dome.

5. How do Lock Screen Widgets & Circle sync work?

Add the Wick Candle widget from your iOS Lock Screen or Home Screen picker. Pair with friends in the Circle tab using iCloud Share (make sure you are signed into iCloud in iOS Settings).

6. How do I toggle Breeze Shake, Lantern Mode, or Match-Strike Sound?

Open the Sanctuary tab to customize your background theme, Wake/Sleep hours, daily step goal, Lantern Mode (100% Glow), Live Breeze Shake, and Sound Effects.
