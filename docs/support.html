Wick Support

Need help with steps, the lock screen, a rest day, or a Spark? Find quick answers below.

1. How do I light my candle in the morning?

Take 5 steps. The candle says your wick is lit. If steps are off, the lighter is there. Tap that line to open Settings. Also turn on Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Wick → Steps and Motion & Fitness.

2. What if I sit still?

The line under your steps names the next walk, up to 10 minutes. After an hour with no steps, a few drops fall and the flame gets smaller. A quiet rain plays if Sound is on. One step does not stop it. Finish that walk, and the rain stops, the flame grows back, and the candle says it waited. The next morning it says it kept yesterday. Your friend sees “Alex is in the rain” in Circle and can walk 500 steps with Ask for Spark.

3. Does Wick work with Apple Watch and update live?

Yes. Wick streams live step-by-step updates via CoreMotion while open and uses Apple HealthKit as its single source of truth to merge iPhone and Apple Watch steps without double-counting.

4. Why does blowing shake the flame during the day without putting it out?

During daytime hours, blowing gently makes the flame flutter like a real breeze. Wick only extinguishes into Night Mode on a strong blow during your configured bedtime window (or when you long-press the candle → Blow Out for Night).

5. How do I start or end a rest day?

Hold the candle, then tap Start a rest day. The first one is free and pauses the candle for a day. Another one is in Wick Pro. To end it early, hold the candle and tap End rest day.

6. How do Lock Screen Widgets & Circle sync work?

Add the Wick Candle widget from your iOS Lock Screen or Home Screen picker. It shows your candle and streak. To see a friend, open the Circle tab, tap Invite, and send one iCloud link. Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud. See the visual invite guide →

7. Where are the look, the name, and sleep?

Open Sanctuary. Look, name, goal, wake, sleep, sound, and the brighter flame are on that screen.
